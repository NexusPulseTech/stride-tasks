How it works

  1. Type one line.
    Review chapter 3 ~45m @daily !pin creates a pinned, daily, 45-minute task.
  2. Start a Pomodoro.
    Work in focused blocks; every session is logged to your deep-work history.
  3. Review on time.
    When a review comes up, pick how it went and Stride schedules the next one at a growing SM-2 interval, so knowledge sticks.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There is no sign-up, even for PRO.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in the browser or desktop app storage. You can export everything to a JSON file at any time.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the app works without internet, and you can install it like a native app.

What is spaced repetition (SM-2)?

A method that schedules reviews at growing intervals, right before you would forget. Stride applies it to tasks and study topics.

How does sync keep my data private?

Your devices encrypt everything with a key derived from your sync code. The server stores only encrypted data it cannot read.
