14-day refund, no questions asked

If Stride Tasks PRO is not right for you, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase. You do not need to give a reason.

How to request a refund

  • Card payments: reply to your receipt email from our Merchant of Record, or email support@nexuspulsetech.xyz with your order number or the email used at checkout.
  • VietQR bank transfers: email support@nexuspulsetech.xyz with the transfer time, amount and order code (starts with STR). We send the money back to the account it came from.

Processing time

We process refund requests within 3 business days. The time for the money to reach you depends on your bank or card issuer, typically 5–10 business days.

After a refund

The license is revoked for the sync service. Your tasks stay on your device and the free version keeps working.
